{% comment %} GALERIA signet, rebuilt from the logo construction in the styleguide: a centre ring and four arcs, split the way the logo is constructed. The stem of the "g" belongs to the bottom arc, the small joint to the right arc, so there are no seams. Every part is a stroke, so it can be drawn in and coloured on its own. The signet is always shown whole, never built up from its parts or rotated. Params: class (size), color (default currentColor), id (data-galeria-logo value for scripts), opacity (for the signet only, so the lines over it stay crisp), sketch-width (line weight), sketch (true adds the construction lines: five circles and a square, drawn in by the script). {% endcomment %}
