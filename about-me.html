Hi, I’m Tara.

Brand Designer and Art Director, based in Düsseldorf. I design brands that feel considered, and I collect the things that inspire them along the way.

After design school, I spent six years at a strategy-focused design agency, working my way from intern to award-winning Senior Art Director. I loved the craft and the team. But I wanted more room to explore.

So I left to live abroad for a while. It taught me that good ideas don’t come from a desk. They come from noticing: the light in a street, the type on a shop sign, the way a place makes you feel.

Today my studio is based in Düsseldorf. I work with companies directly and join agency teams as a freelance Art Director. And I still travel whenever I can.
