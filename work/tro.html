Brand Design, 2025

The challenge

Our world is loud. Images, messages and noise, all at once. TRO creates sound for brands that brings the pieces back together.

TRO is an agency for sound branding and audio communication in Düsseldorf. Their new brand needed to do what their work does: turn chaos into harmony, and make people want to stay.

The idea we landed on: TRO are the Creators of Symphonic Worlds. Sound that surrounds, connects and creates meaning. My task was to make that sound visible.

The logo

Let sound live. Now.

The logo became more than a logo: a switch, a moment to turn sound on. Its bar stretches to any format, and the circle travels along it like a play button or a progress bar.

Design concept

Symphonic Worlds. Bold. Vibrant. For real.

The sound TRO creates is more than what you can hear. So the identity turns it into something you can see and almost touch: large, glowing spheres that fill the space and change like a melody.

Infographics

Data that sounds like TRO.

Soft spheres meet thin, precise lines and technical annotations. The contrast between organic and technical makes complex information easy to read, from frequency charts to tempo and share.

Design system

One sphere, endless layouts.

The sphere moves freely within any format: centered, cropped, pushed to the edge or reduced to a single arc in the corner. On light and dark backgrounds, it creates tension, depth and rhythm, while the logo bar always anchors the layout.

Designed to be heard.
