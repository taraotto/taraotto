The challenge

GVV is not one of the big insurers, but it is close to its customers. Its brand kept its distance.

The Cologne insurer has one foot in municipal insurance and one in private customers. Its private branch wanted to be seen more clearly, become better known and stand apart from its municipal sister.

As Senior Art Director at KW43 Branddesign in Düsseldorf, I looked after GVV. Starting from a brand fit check, we modernised the brand as a whole, then brought it to a wide range of media.

The name

GVV Privat
GVV Direkt

"Privat" sounded exclusive and made people think of private health insurance. "Direkt" is clear and concise, says what kind of insurer this is and points to the personal connection with its customers.

Brand architecture

One family, two directions.

Under the umbrella of GVV Versicherungen, GVV Direkt and GVV Kommunal stand side by side. The established blue-green of the umbrella splits up: blue for Direkt, green for Kommunal.

Design language

Insurance, in plain words.

GVV speaks plainly: uncomplicated, direct and with concrete messages. So the brand talks in dialog boxes, as if you were texting with someone who knows their stuff. They make every headline a small conversation, with a wink.

Typography

Friendly, and easy to read.

The old condensed Frutiger was factual and saved space, but it was harder to read and hard to warm to. Montserrat is built from the same geometry as the new letters in the logo, and made for screens.
