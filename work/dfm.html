Disgusting Food Museum
Brand Design, Art Direction, 2022

The challenge

A museum full of food most people would never eat. Its brand had to make the repulsive look almost delicious.

The Disgusting Food Museum challenges visitors to rethink what they find disgusting, and even invites them to try a few dishes at its daring tasting bar.

What disgusts one person might delight another. Together with my former KW43 colleagues Alexander Geh and Rüdiger Goetz, I created a brand that turns the repulsive into something enticing. disgustingfoodmuseum.com

Fermented shark, maggot-infested cheese, mouse wine or the infamous durian are just a few of the exhibits at the Disgusting Food Museum.

The idea

Every language has its own yuck.

While we collected ideas, we noticed that every language has its own way of saying "disgusting". In the spirit of a museum that travels the world, these words became the heart of the brand: the logo speaks the language of each place it visits.

The look

Disgusting, but make it delicious.

What disgusts one person might delight another. So instead of showing the gross, the brand looks like a friendly diner: fresh colours, everyday tableware and packaging, and a logo you would trust with your lunch. The posters for the exhibition in Los Angeles, one of the stops on its tour, make you hungry before you think twice.

Tasting bar

Brave enough for a bite?

At the tasting bar, visitors try a selection of unusual foods. The challenge: resist the urge to gag, or be counted on the notorious vomit board.

On tour

From Malmö to the world.

Rooted in Malmö as a permanent exhibition, the Disgusting Food Museum has travelled far, from Los Angeles to Berlin and many other cities, always speaking the local language.
