The challenge

A new dental practice in an old half-timbered house in Braunschweig. Before the doors opened, it needed a strategy, a name, a brand and a website.

Dentist Carolin Hochberger was opening her own practice. Not a cold clinic, but a place people like to come back to, and where the team feels at home too.

I accompanied the whole way: from a shared design strategy and the search for the right name to the logo, the brand guide and the website.

The house

A house with a story.

The practice moved into a well-preserved half-timbered house from the 18th century at Hutfiltern 6, a piece of Braunschweig's history. Its past shaped the brand from the start. An old map of the city, with the house right in it, later became the wallpaper of the practice.

Strategy

Sustainable dental health for a new way of feeling good.

Zahnfachwerk puts well-being and long-term health first. Excellent quality, talking at eye level and digital innovation, in rooms that feel calm for patients and team alike. Four values carry it all.

Inspiration

Old walls, soft greens.

The moodboard is the result of the strategy: elegance through understatement, warm tones and fine details, with more of the old building's character. It serves as inspiration and gave the client and me a shared foundation before the first sketch.

The signet

A tooth, a house and three letters.

The outer shape is a stylised tooth. Its lines follow the beams of a half-timbered house, a sign of quality and permanence. And hidden inside are the letters Z, F and W.

Logo

Precise, with soft corners.

Geometric capitals with a special touch: subtle roundings pick up the details of the signet. Clear lines and soft contours, precision and warmth. For round formats, the name wraps around the signet together with the city.

Typography

History meets clarity.

Libre Baskerville has roots going back to 1750 and recalls the listed building. Outfit is clear and modern, made for reading. Together they connect history and today.

Design language

Lines like a timber frame.

Inspired by the clear, geometric structure of a half-timbered house, thin lines divide every layout into fields, like beams divide a facade. Now and then a diagonal brace adds an accent.

The practice

Old house, new life.

Wooden floors, high windows and calm colours. The old city map covers the staircase, the signet hangs at the end of the corridor and appears on the team's scrubs.
