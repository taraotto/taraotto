Brand Design, Art Direction, 2025

The challenge

Money moves around the world all day. A young fintech wanted it to feel as light as the sky.

Bloxley is building an all-in-one money app: one account, a card and payments across currencies, made to feel simple and effortless.

Together with my strategic partner Rüdiger Goetz, I developed the brand concept and the logo. After that, I was responsible for the brand, with a focus on colour, cards and imagery.

Cards

One card, every sky.

Each card carries a soft gradient from the palette, with the signet as the only mark. Five shades for everyday banking, from the light breeze to the deep midnight.

Colour

Every mood of the sky.

The palette is inspired by the ever-changing sky. A gradient from deep to light blue carries the brand, azure adds contrast and midnight blue is for text. Breeze, sunset and sunrise bring the sky's moods.

Imagery

Always looking up.

People in motion, seen from below against open skies. The images are bright, natural and full of light, so that money feels like freedom rather than a chore.

Social media

Light words, big skies.

Short lowercase lines on bright photos, the signet small in the corner. The posts introduce the brand before launch, sometimes with a wink, sometimes with a glimpse of the app.
