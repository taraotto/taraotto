When famous German institutions Karstadt and Galeria Kaufhof merged, they aimed for a branding that would communicate the joy of shopping in a department store full of possibilities.

The result is a memorable and precise logo that embodies happiness and variety. It was created together with my fellow designers and colleagues at KW43 Branddesign.
