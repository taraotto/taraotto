Brand Design, 2021

The challenge

Two German department store icons became one. The new brand had to bring back the joy of shopping.

When Karstadt and Galeria Kaufhof merged, they needed an identity for a department store full of possibilities: modern, warm and open to everyone, in the heart of the city.

The result is a precise, memorable signet that stands for happiness and variety. I created it with my colleagues at KW43 Branddesign: Jürgen Adolph, Marc Schäde and Alexandra Sobota.

The signet

Five circles and a square.

Precise geometry with a soft, friendly result. And in it, many small messages:

  • A flower,
  • a soft cloud,
  • a playful smile,
  • and at its heart, the city.

Colors

Fifteen tones, one inviting spectrum.

Five dark, five bright and five light tones that combine into strong contrasts, so the brand can show up in every color and still feel like one.

Campaign

From poster to shopping bag.

Posters, the customer card campaign, the digital magazine, flyers and bags share one confident look: bright tones from the palette, real people up front and the signet as a clear sender.
